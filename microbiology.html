The big idea

What does microbiology study?

Microbiology is the branch of biology that studies microorganisms—organisms and biological agents so small that many can only be seen with a microscope. These include bacteria, many fungi, microscopic algae, and protozoa.

Microorganisms are found almost everywhere: in soil, water, and air, and on or inside living things. Some can cause disease, while many others are harmless or helpful.

Why it matters

Small organisms, big roles

Microorganisms help break down dead material, support nutrient cycles, and are used to make foods such as yogurt. Microbiology also helps people understand infectious diseases and improve hygiene and health practices.

✦

Stay safe: Learning about microbes is fascinating, but never collect or grow unknown samples. Use microscope slides and experiments prepared by a teacher.
