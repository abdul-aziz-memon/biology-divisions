The big idea

What does zoology study?

Zoology is a branch of biology that studies animals. Zoologists investigate how animals are built, how they behave, how they grow, and how they interact with other living things and their surroundings.

Animals range from tiny insects to enormous whales. Studying them helps us understand the variety of life on Earth and the connections that keep ecosystems working.

A closer look

What do zoologists explore?

01

Animal structure

Body parts, movement, and the ways animals are adapted to their environments.

02

Behavior

How animals find food, communicate, care for young, and respond to change.

03

Habitats

Where animals live and how they depend on other organisms in an ecosystem.

04

Classification

How scientists group animals by shared features and evolutionary relationships.

Why it matters

Understanding and protecting wildlife

Zoology can help people care for wildlife, protect habitats, and understand the effects of environmental changes. Careful observation and evidence help scientists make better decisions for animal populations.
