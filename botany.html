The big idea

What does botany study?

Botany is a branch of biology focused on plants. Botanists study how plants are structured, how they grow and reproduce, and how they respond to their environments.

Plants include flowering plants, grasses, ferns, mosses, and many other groups. They make their own food using sunlight, and they provide food and habitats for countless organisms.

A closer look

What do botanists explore?

01

Plant parts

Roots, stems, leaves, and flowers—and the jobs each part does.

02

Growth

03

Reproduction

How plants make seeds or spores and spread to new places.

04

Plant communities

How plants interact with animals, fungi, and their surroundings.

Why it matters

Plants make life possible

Plants are important sources of food, materials, and habitats. They also take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen during photosynthesis. Learning about plants helps people grow crops and protect forests and other ecosystems.
